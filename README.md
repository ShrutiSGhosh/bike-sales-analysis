# 🚲 Bike Dekho – Bike Sales Analysis

A complete Excel-based data analysis project that explores customer purchase behavior for bikes.  
Conducted using Microsoft Excel with data cleaning, pivot tables, dashboards, and business insights.

---

## 📁 Project Deliverables

- ✅ Cleaned Dataset (`Cleaned_Bike_Data.xlsx`)
- ✅ Pivot Tables & Visuals (`Bike_Dekho_Dashboard.xlsx`)
- ✅ Dashboard (Interactive with Slicers and Charts)
- ✅ PowerPoint Presentation (`Bike_Sales_Analysis_Presentation.pptx`)
- ✅ 🎥 [Video Walkthrough – Watch here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1fSJp3o9oGhJO1-jbcEWHPKFWGUimOiDD/view?usp=sharing) *(Hosted on Google Drive)*

---

## 📊 Tools & Skills Used

- Microsoft Excel (Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers)
- Dashboard Design
- Customer Segmentation
- Business Insight Generation

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Region:** Highest purchases from North America
- **Demographics:** Mid-income, females, and age group 30–45 show highest purchase rates
- **Commute:** Short-distance commuters (2–5 mi) purchase more bikes
- **Car Ownership:** Households with 0–1 cars have significantly higher bike purchase rates

---

## 📌 Objective

To analyze customer demographics and behaviors to identify target segments most likely to purchase bikes, thereby supporting more focused marketing strategies.
